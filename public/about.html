About

Synexis provides systems integration and data infrastructure services for organizations that require precision and reliability.

The Firm

Synexis was founded to address a gap in technical services: organizations with complex systems requirements often face a choice between large consultancies with high overhead and limited accountability, or smaller firms with narrow technical capabilities.

We offer an alternative. Synexis combines deep technical expertise with the focus and accountability of a specialist firm. We take on a limited number of engagements to ensure each receives appropriate attention. We measure success by operational outcomes, not billable hours.

Our clients typically operate in regulated industries or manage critical infrastructure. They require systems that work correctly under real conditions, with documentation and knowledge transfer that enables long-term independence.
